\section{Conclusion}
\jama{Rewritten from the JAMA conclusion; claims limited to what the results show.}
We formulated prehospital stroke routing as an MDP over patient location, time since onset and subtype uncertainty, with capability-classified hospitals, road travel times, inter-facility transfer and published in-hospital intervals, and solved it online within the dispatch window. In simulation across two regions the policy improved expected outcomes over nearest-hospital routing, matched a centralised bypass protocol while distributing load, and was robust to travel-time error and in-hospital interval assumptions. A shallow decision tree recovered \pending{xx}\% of the gain and requires no computation to apply. The hospital table and routing engine are inputs, so the approach transfers to other regions by changing data. Capacity modelling and validation against observed outcomes are the next steps before deployment.
